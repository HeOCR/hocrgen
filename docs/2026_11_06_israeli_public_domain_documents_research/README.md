# Israeli public-domain documents: source research (2026-11-06)

Status: Raw research, archived verbatim  
Last updated: 2026-11-06  
Primary audience: agents and maintainers planning non-synthetic (real) source intake

[`gemini_conversation.md`](./gemini_conversation.md) is a verbatim backup of a
Gemini conversation the maintainer held on 2026-11-06. It is kept unedited, so
that later source proposals can cite what was asked and answered. The title is
in Hebrew; it translates as "Copyright and uses of government tender documents".
The conversation covers:

1. whether tender documents published by the Israeli Government Procurement
   Administration (mr.gov.il) are public domain, and which licences or legal
   exceptions apply to them;
2. the open tender datasets on data.gov.il;
3. whether state documents older than 50 years are public domain;
4. where to bulk-download Hebrew public-domain documents (born-digital, scanned
   print and scanned handwriting);
5. which of those collections also ship full text or OCR alongside the images.

## How to use it

- **This is AI output, not legal advice and not a rights decision.** Every claim
  in it is `Needs verification` against the primary source (the statute, the
  portal's terms page, the collection's per-item rights statement) before it
  backs any source record.
- Rights and licence calls on any source are the maintainer's. Nothing here
  changes `profile_open_v1` eligibility or any entry in
  `src/hocrgen/config/sources.yaml`.
- A collection named here still enters hocrgen the usual way: a source proposal
  issue, then a narrow, fixture-backed adapter PR. See
  [`../source_adapter_contribution_guide.md`](../source_adapter_contribution_guide.md).
- Handwritten candidates also belong to HASH's scope. Its own source research
  notes live in `HeOCR/hash` under `docs/sources/`.

## Points to check first *(inferred, from a read of the conversation)*

- Some links are not direct. The Reshumot link is wrapped in a Google search
  URL, and one data.gov.il resource ID is truncated.
- Several claims are broad and need per-item checks: that nearly all of Ktiv is
  public domain, that Wikimedia Commons files have already been rights-filtered,
  and how widely §19 (fair use) and §§20–21 (works open to public inspection)
  reach.
- §5 (facts and data) and §6 (official publications) of the Copyright Act
  2007 are the main grounds it cites for born-digital material. §42 (50-year
  term for state works) and §43 (term ends at the close of the calendar year)
  are the grounds for older state files.
